# Kinemor Brand System

## Foundation

Kinemor is the evidence layer for Physical AI. The identity should feel precise, calm, and quietly ambitious: closer to a research instrument than an advertising campaign.

## Selected Mark: Kinetic Aperture

The selected Kinemor mark is an abstract, open `K` built from one vertical observation rail and two outgoing paths. It suggests an aperture opening from perception into action. The lime center is a single confirmed observation.

Use the mark alone for app icons, favicons, hardware, social avatars, and small spaces. Use `Kinemor` set in Geist for the standard lockup. Do not rotate, outline, add a gradient, or place it on busy imagery.

## Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Graphite | `#111213` | Main dark field, footer, high-confidence communications |
| Foundation | Ivory | `#F2F0EA` | Main light surface and text on dark surfaces |
| Field | Deep Field | `#171A1A` | Data visualisation surfaces |
| Accent | Signal Lime | `#C8FF3D` | Calls to action, selected states, confirmed signal only |
| Support | Moss | `#7F9B92` | Secondary technical metadata |
| Support | Warm Silver | `#C9CCC4` | Dividers and restrained secondary UI |

Signal Lime should remain below roughly 8% of a composition. Its scarcity is part of its meaning.

## Typography

- Display and body: Geist Sans, medium-weight headlines, comfortable line height in body copy.
- Metadata: Geist Mono, uppercase only where a small operational label benefits from it.
- Avoid compressed, futuristic display fonts. Kinemor should be legible and durable, not performatively technical.

## Layout and Motion

- Use a 12-column layout with real whitespace and one clear hierarchy per view.
- Treat content sections as bands and editorial grids, not floating card piles.
- Use hairline dividers, concise labels, and evidence-like data visuals.
- Motion should reveal hierarchy or signal state. It must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and never delay reading.
- Use glass only for transient navigation over media. Do not turn every surface into frosted glass.

## Voice

Precise, concrete, and future-facing. Prefer `real environments`, `multimodal context`, and `accountable data` to vague AI superlatives.
